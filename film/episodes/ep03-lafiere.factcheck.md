The causeway is about 500 yards in CMH and HN and 500 metres in DDO; the line follows the two that agree.
CMH says the smoke screen was too thin, HN that there was none, so the film does not mention smoke.

## Stand-in

dod_merderet is named for the river and built around a stone bridge over it, so it stands in for La Fière.
In the map the bridge is short and runs north-south over a channel; the real causeway was a long embanked
road across flooded meadows. The narration describes the real ground; the pictures are a recreation.

## ADR

Generic and invented, no real person named or voiced. The whispered "Who's there?" exchange is invented and
deliberately avoids quoting the D-Day challenge and password, which this check could not source. "Go! Go! Go!" is the signal HN records at 10:45 on 9 June, spoken by an unnamed captain, not presented as
any real person's voice. The German "Panzer vor!" is subtitled.
