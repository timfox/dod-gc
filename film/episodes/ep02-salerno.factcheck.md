## Stand-in

dod_avalanche is set by its authors near Salerno in September 1943 (map briefing: the 13 September
counterattack, towns changing hands). Its square and church stand in for a hill village like Altavilla; it
is not a model of Altavilla.

## ADR

Generic and invented, no real person named or voiced. The 36th Division was a Texas National Guard division,
so the American voices are Texan or Southwestern. The radio call follows period procedure. German lines are
plain spoken German, subtitled.
