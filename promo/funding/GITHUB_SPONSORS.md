## What GitHub needs from you (you must do this yourself)
1. Enable 2FA on your GitHub account (required before approval).
2. Go to https://github.com/sponsors → Get sponsored on your personal account.
3. Choose payouts via **Stripe Connect bank account** (US region matches US bank) or a fiscal host. Fiscal host can only be set at signup.
4. Complete Stripe Connect with legal name, DOB, address, bank account — identity details are hard to change after submit.
5. Submit US tax form **W-9** in the Sponsors dashboard.
6. Publish tiers, then **Request approval**. GitHub review can take a few days; profile goes live when approved.
7. After approval, update `.github/FUNDING.yml` in the repo (draft lives in this folder; do not commit until you say so).
