## Short bio
I’m Tim Fox. I spend my nights porting Half-Life 1 and Day of Defeat to the Nintendo GameCube through the community Xash3D-GC engine (`timfox/xash3d-gc`). Progress is slow, honest, and public — broken geometry, memory limits, skyboxes, menus, whatever the build can do that week.

Support here goes to my time and the engine work itself. It is not payment for Valve games, assets, or builds of the games. Half-Life and Day of Defeat belong to Valve. You still need your own legal copies.

Project hub: https://gamecubehalf.life  
Source: https://github.com/timfox/xash3d-gc

## What your support funds
- Evenings spent on the GX renderer, memory limits, and map bring-up
- Capture, editing, and honest progress videos
- Keeping the public repo, site, and Discord updates going
- Occasional hardware / storage costs for the ports

What it does **not** buy:
- Game builds, WADs, maps, music, or any Valve content
- Early access to pirated or redistributed game files
- A license to Half-Life or Day of Defeat
